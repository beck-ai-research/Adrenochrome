## 1. Description
The adrenochrome conspiracy theory represents a modern evolution of historical antisemitic blood libel myths. Traditional blood libels accused Jewish people of kidnapping Christian children to drink their blood; the modern version replaces "blood" with "adrenochrome harvesting" while maintaining the same core antisemitic structure.

## 2. Historical & Extremist Context
- **Historical Precursor:** 12th-19th century European blood libel accusations against Jewish communities
- **1903 Influence:** Protocols of the Elders of Zion influencing modern "Deep State" myths
- **Modern Adoption:** 2013-2014 4chan /pol/ threads explicitly linking adrenochrome to "Jew Ritual BLOOD LIBEL Sacrifice"
- **Target Audience:** QAnon followers, neo-Nazi groups, conspiracy communities

## 3. Dissemination Channels
- **Platforms:** 4chan /x/ and /pol/ boards (2013-2014), Pizzagate communities (2016), QAnon (2017+)
- **Key Amplifiers:** Hunter S. Thompson's fictional work, conspiracy filmmakers, social media influencers
- **Evolution Path:** Traditional antisemitism -> Pizzagate -> QAnon -> COVID-19 pandemic surge

## 4. Verification & Fact-Check
- **Claimed "Fact":** Jewish people harvest adrenochrome from children
- **Veritas Findings:** 
  - Direct continuation of historical blood libel antisemitism
  - First appeared on 4chan /pol/ in 2013-2014 explicitly as "Jew Ritual BLOOD LIBEL"
