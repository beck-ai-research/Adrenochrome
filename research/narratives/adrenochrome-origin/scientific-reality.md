## 1. Description
Adrenochrome is a chemical compound with formula C9H9NO3, produced by oxidation of adrenaline (epinephrine). It is an unstable, pink solution that further oxidizes to brown/black melanin compounds. The compound has been studied since the 1950s for potential connections to schizophrenia but has no proven medical applications.

## 2. Historical & Scientific Context
- **Historical Precursor:** Scientific research dating back to 1856 when French physician Alfred Vulpian observed epinephrine turning reddish when exposed to air
- **Modern Research:** 1937 isolation by David Ezra Green and Derek Richter; 1950s-60s schizophrenia research by Hoffer and Osmond
- **Scientific Consensus:** No proven medical uses, not a controlled substance, synthesized commercially since 1952
