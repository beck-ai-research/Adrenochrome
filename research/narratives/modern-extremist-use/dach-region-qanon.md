## 1. Overview
QAnon is an online subculture spreading conspiracy narratives with connections to antisemitism, right-wing extremism, and anti-state delegitimization. Since approximately 2018, QAnon narratives have spread among German-speaking internet users with references to German politics and society, particularly during the COVID-19 pandemic.

## 2. Extremist Connections
- **Primary Affiliation:** QAnon, Reichsbürger, Neo-Nazi movements
- **Adrenochrome Usage:** Core element of QAnon mythology about "pedophile elite" harvesting children
- **Network Nodes:** Integration with Reichsbürger movements using "WWG1WGA" slogans

## 3. OSINT Data (Chrome DevTools MCP)
- **Domain(s):** Various German-language alternative media platforms and social media channels
- **Infrastructure:** Decentralized social media networks, encrypted messaging apps
- **Metadata Findings:** Shared slogans, cross-platform narrative amplification
