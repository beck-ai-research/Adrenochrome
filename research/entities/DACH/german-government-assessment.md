## 1. Overview
Based on the German Bundestag's response to parliamentary inquiry (19/24084), the German federal government officially recognizes QAnon as a dangerous conspiracy theory originating in the USA. The assessment highlights QAnon's connection to antisemitism, right-wing extremism, and anti-state delegitimization, warranting monitoring by intelligence services.
