## Key German-Specific Findings

### BKA Assessment (2021)
**Holger Münch, BKA President:**
- "Wir sehen mit Sorge, dass die Zahl der Bedrohungen und Anfeindungen zunimmt"
- QAnon developed during COVID-19 pandemic, particularly during lockdown protests
- German politicians being targeted by conspiracy narratives
- Prominent figures amplifying QAnon content in German media

### Psychological Analysis (Kriminalpolizei 2024)
**Marcel Auber, Ludwigsburg:**
- QAnon represents "gute alte Kampf von Gut gegen Böse"
- Small conspiratorial group claiming to know "the truth"
- Psychological pattern: Creating simple explanations for complex world events
- Development psychology: Conspiracy theories provide certainty in uncertain times

- **Cultural Adaptation:** German-specific narratives targeting German institutions
